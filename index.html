Expedition Command – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Expedition Command is a Garmin Connect IQ watch face. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little information as possible.

1. Data used on your Garmin device

The watch face may access data provided by Garmin Connect IQ APIs in order to display watch-face information and features. Depending on your device and selected modules, this may include time and date, battery level, steps, heart rate, calories, active minutes, altitude, recovery information, notifications count, weather information, sunrise/sunset information, position-related data and other device-provided metrics.

This information is used primarily on the watch to render the selected watch-face fields. The developer does not operate a separate server that collects or stores your health, activity or location history.

2. KiezelPay / KPay licensing and payments

Expedition Command uses KiezelPay (KPay) to provide a free trial and paid activation. KiezelPay may process information required for licensing, activation, fraud prevention and payment processing, such as an activation code, device/app identifiers, transaction status and other information required by its service.

The developer does not receive or store your full credit-card number or complete banking information. Payment processing is handled by KiezelPay and its payment providers under their own privacy terms.

3. Trial and purchase

The intended offer for Expedition Command is an 8-hour free trial followed by a one-time unlock price of US$2.99. Trial duration and pricing are managed through KiezelPay and may be shown on the KiezelPay purchase page.

4. Internet and communications

Network communication may be used when required by Garmin services, weather features, or KiezelPay licensing and activation. Expedition Command does not use this permission to create a separate advertising or behavioral-tracking profile.

5. Data sharing

The developer does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by Garmin or KiezelPay only as necessary to provide their respective platform, licensing, payment or device services.

6. Data retention

The developer does not maintain a separate cloud database of your watch-face health, activity or location data. Licensing or purchase records handled by KiezelPay are subject to KiezelPay's own retention practices.

7. Children

Expedition Command is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, Garmin platform requirements or third-party licensing services change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

9. Contact

For questions about Expedition Command, please use the developer contact option provided on the Garmin Connect IQ Store listing.

Expedition Command – 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 16 日

Expedition Command 是 Garmin Connect IQ 錶面。我們重視您的隱私，並以最少資料使用為原則。

1. Garmin 裝置上的資料

為了顯示錶面資訊與功能，本錶面可能透過 Garmin Connect IQ API 使用裝置提供的資料。依裝置與您選擇的模組不同，可能包括日期與時間、電池電量、步數、心率、卡路里、活動分鐘、海拔、恢復資訊、通知數量、天氣、日出／日落、位置相關資料及其他 Garmin 裝置提供的指標。

這些資料主要在手錶上用於顯示您選擇的錶面欄位。開發者不經營額外的伺服器來收集或儲存您的健康、活動或位置歷史紀錄。

2. KiezelPay / KPay 授權與付款

Expedition Command 使用 KiezelPay（KPay）提供免費試用與付費解鎖。KiezelPay 可能為授權、啟用、防止詐欺及付款處理之必要用途，處理啟用碼、裝置／App 識別資訊、交易狀態及其服務所需的其他資料。

開發者不會取得或儲存您的完整信用卡號或完整銀行資料。付款由 KiezelPay 及其付款服務供應商依其各自的隱私政策處理。

3. 試用與購買

Expedition Command 預計提供 8 小時免費試用，試用結束後可一次性支付 US$2.99 解鎖。實際試用時間與售價由 KiezelPay 後台設定，並可能顯示於 KiezelPay 購買頁面。

4. 網路與通訊

當 Garmin 服務、天氣功能或 KiezelPay 授權／啟用需要時，App 可能使用網路通訊。Expedition Command 不會利用此權限建立獨立的廣告或行為追蹤資料。

5. 資料分享

開發者不販售個人資料。Garmin 或 KiezelPay 可能僅在提供其平台、授權、付款或裝置服務所需範圍內處理相關資料。

6. 資料保存

開發者不建立另外的雲端資料庫來保存您的錶面健康、活動或位置資料。由 KiezelPay 處理的授權或購買紀錄，依 KiezelPay 自身的資料保存政策處理。

7. 兒童隱私

Expedition Command 並非設計用來透過開發者自營服務主動蒐集兒童個人資料。

8. 政策變更

若 App 功能、Garmin 平台要求或第三方授權服務有所變更，本政策可能更新。如有重大變更，將同步更新本頁最上方日期。

9. 聯絡方式

如對 Expedition Command 有任何疑問，請使用 Garmin Connect IQ Store 商品頁中的「聯絡開發者」功能。
